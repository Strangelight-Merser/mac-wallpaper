**The first public release of MacWallpaper: your Wallpaper Engine wallpapers, alive on your Mac.** Scenes, videos and
web wallpapers from your own Steam account, played natively with Metal. ([简体中文说明见下方](#简体中文))

## Highlights

- **Your wallpapers, from your account.** Log in with Steam's QR code: MacWallpaper downloads Wallpaper Engine's files
  and your subscriptions, and keeps them up to date. Folders copied from a Windows PC work too.
- **Browse the Workshop in the app.** Trending, popular, search and filters; subscribing downloads the wallpaper,
  with its size shown first and small previews to save data.
- **Rich scenes.** Effects, particles, text and clocks, sound, audio visualisation, SceneScript, puppet animation,
  lights and volumetric light, presets and your own pictures. Plus videos (H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9, VP8) and web
  wallpapers.
- **Feels like a Mac app.** A menu-bar app with a settings window, live per-wallpaper settings, open at login, and
  smart pausing: covered desktop, locked screen, sleeping displays, battery or Low Power Mode.
- **"Dusk" included**, a wallpaper of our own, so there's something to play right away.

## Install

1. Download **MacWallpaper-0.1.0.zip** below, unzip it and move **MacWallpaper** to Applications.
2. Open it once. This build isn't notarized, so macOS blocks it: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and
   click **Open Anyway**.
3. The welcome guide takes you through Steam login, Wallpaper Engine's files and choosing wallpapers.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and
[Wallpaper Engine](https://store.steampowered.com/app/431960/) on your Steam account.

## Known limitations

- 3D model objects in scenes aren't drawn yet (the library marks wallpapers that use them).
- Video wallpapers play without sound.
- Every display shows the same wallpaper.
- No automatic updates yet: watch this repository's releases to hear about new versions.

## 简体中文

**MacWallpaper 的第一个公开版本：让你的 Wallpaper Engine 壁纸，在 Mac 上动起来。** 播放你自己 Steam 账号里的场景、
视频和网页壁纸，用 Metal 原生渲染。

### 亮点

- **用你自己账号里的壁纸。** 用 Steam 扫码登录，MacWallpaper 会下载 Wallpaper Engine 的文件和你订阅的壁纸，并保持
  更新。从 Windows 电脑拷来的文件夹也能用。
- **在应用里逛创意工坊。** 趋势、热门、搜索和筛选；订阅即下载，先显示大小，预览图只取小图以节省流量。
- **丰富的场景效果。** 特效、粒子、文字和时钟、声音、随音乐律动、SceneScript 脚本、木偶动画、灯光和体积光、预设和
  自定义图片。还有视频壁纸（H.264、HEVC、AV1、VP9、VP8）和网页壁纸。
- **地道的 Mac 应用。** 菜单栏应用，带设置窗口；每个壁纸的设置实时生效；开机自启；桌面被遮挡、锁屏、显示器睡眠、
  用电池或低电量模式时都能自动暂停。
- **随附壁纸“Dusk”**，我们自己做的，装好就能用。

### 安装

1. 下载下面的 **MacWallpaper-0.1.0.zip**，解压后把 **MacWallpaper** 拖进“应用程序”。
2. 先打开一次。这个版本没有经过公证，会被 macOS 拦下：到**系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**里点**仍要打开**。
3. 欢迎页会带你登录 Steam、获取 Wallpaper Engine 的文件并挑选壁纸。

需要搭载 Apple 芯片的 Mac、macOS 14 Sonoma 或更新版本，以及 Steam 账号里的
[Wallpaper Engine](https://store.steampowered.com/app/431960/)。

### 已知限制

- 场景里的 3D 模型对象暂时不会显示（壁纸库会标出用到它的壁纸）。
- 视频壁纸暂时没有声音。
- 所有显示器显示同一个壁纸。
- 暂时不能自动更新：可以在仓库里关注（Watch → Releases）以获知新版本。
