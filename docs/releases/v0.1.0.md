The first public release of MacWallpaper: your Wallpaper Engine wallpapers, on your Mac.

**Requirements:** a Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and [Wallpaper Engine](https://store.steampowered.com/app/431960/) on your Steam account.

## Install

1. Download **MacWallpaper-0.1.0.zip** below, unzip it and move MacWallpaper to Applications.
2. Open it. This build isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 you can also right-click the app and choose **Open**).
3. The welcome guide takes you through logging in to Steam, getting Wallpaper Engine's files and picking wallpapers from your subscriptions.

## What's in it

- **Your wallpapers, from your account:** log in to Steam with a QR code; MacWallpaper downloads Wallpaper Engine's files and your subscriptions, and keeps them up to date. Or use folders copied from a Windows PC.
- **Browse the Workshop** in the app: trending, popular, search, filters; subscribe and download, with the size shown first and small previews to save data.
- **Scenes** with effects, particles, text and clocks, sound, audio visualisation, SceneScript, puppet animation, lights and volumetric light, user pictures and presets. **Video** (H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9, VP8) and **web** wallpapers.
- **Settings for each wallpaper**, live; display modes (fill, fit, stretch).
- **A settings window:** open at login, frame rate, pause on battery or in Low Power Mode, sound, audio visualisation, storage.
- **"Dusk"**, a wallpaper of our own, so there's something to play right away.
- English and Simplified Chinese.

## Known limitations

- 3D model objects in scenes aren't drawn yet; the library marks wallpapers that use them.
- Video wallpapers play without sound.
- Every display shows the same wallpaper.
- No automatic app updates yet: check this page for new versions.
- Not notarized (see Install).

`wecli-0.1.0.zip` is the command-line tool for rendering wallpapers to images and inspecting Wallpaper Engine's formats; most people don't need it. `MacWallpaper-0.1.0.sha256` has the checksums.

MacWallpaper is free software under the GPL-3.0. It is not affiliated with Wallpaper Engine Team or Valve and ships no Wallpaper Engine content.

## 简体中文

MacWallpaper 的第一个公开版本：在 Mac 上用你的 Wallpaper Engine 壁纸。

**系统要求：** 搭载 Apple 芯片的 Mac、macOS 14 Sonoma 或更新版本，以及 Steam 账号里的 [Wallpaper Engine](https://store.steampowered.com/app/431960/)。

### 安装

1. 下载下面的 **MacWallpaper-0.1.0.zip**，解压后把 MacWallpaper 拖进“应用程序”文件夹。
2. 打开它。这个版本没有经过 Apple 公证，第一次打开会被 macOS 拦下：到**系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**里点**仍要打开**（macOS 14 上也可以右键点按应用，选择**打开**）。
3. 欢迎页会带你登录 Steam、获取 Wallpaper Engine 的文件，并从你的订阅里挑选壁纸。

### 包含的功能

- **用你自己账号里的壁纸：** 扫码登录 Steam，MacWallpaper 会下载 Wallpaper Engine 的文件和你订阅的壁纸，并保持更新。也可以用从 Windows 电脑拷来的文件夹。
- **在应用里浏览创意工坊：** 趋势、热门、搜索、筛选；订阅即下载，先显示大小，预览图只取小图以节省流量。
- **场景壁纸：** 特效、粒子、文字和时钟、声音、随音乐律动、SceneScript 脚本、木偶动画、灯光和体积光、自定义图片和预设。**视频壁纸**（H.264、HEVC、AV1、VP9、VP8）和**网页壁纸**。
- **每个壁纸的设置**实时生效；显示方式（铺满、完整显示、拉伸）。
- **设置窗口：** 开机自启、帧率、用电池或低电量模式时暂停、声音、随音乐律动、存储空间。
- **随附壁纸“Dusk”**，我们自己做的，装好就能用。
- 支持简体中文和英文。

### 已知限制

- 场景里的 3D 模型对象暂时不会显示；壁纸库会标出用到它的壁纸。
- 视频壁纸暂时没有声音。
- 所有显示器显示同一个壁纸。
- 应用暂时不能自动更新：请回到这个页面查看新版本。
- 没有经过公证（见“安装”）。

`wecli-0.1.0.zip` 是命令行工具，用来把壁纸渲染成图片、检查 Wallpaper Engine 的各种格式，大多数人用不到。`MacWallpaper-0.1.0.sha256` 是校验和。

MacWallpaper 是基于 GPL-3.0 的自由软件，与 Wallpaper Engine 开发团队及 Valve 没有任何关系，也不附带任何 Wallpaper Engine 的内容。
